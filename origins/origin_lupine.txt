*label the_bite_memory

The memory is fever-dream and nightmare:

[i]The forest. The hunt. You were prey—running from something, you can't remember what. Then the massive black wolf, stepping from moonlight. Eyes like amber fire.[/i]

[i]The alpha. Rurik. Judging you with a gaze that saw into your soul.[/i]

[i]His voice, resonating in your mind: "You have the heart of a wolf. Prove it."[/i]

[i]Then—the bite. Not to kill, but to transform. Venom flooding your veins. Three days and nights of agony as your body fought and finally succumbed to the lunar curse.[/i]

[i]On the third night, under the full moon, you shifted for the first time.[/i]

*choice
    #I embraced the beast within
        The wolf felt [i]right[/i]. Natural. Like you'd been wearing a human mask your whole life and finally tore it off.

        *set rage +20
        *set control -10
        *set strength +15
        *goto type_selection

    #I fought to keep my humanity
        The wolf is power, yes—but you are still [i]you[/i]. Human mind, lupine body. You will master this, not be mastered by it.

        *set control +20
        *set rage -10
        *set will +15
        *goto type_selection

    #I accepted it as part of me now
        Neither beast nor man, but something between. The wolf is you, you are the wolf. Two souls, one being.

        *set control +10
        *set rage +5
        *set cunning +10
        *goto type_selection

The pack's eldest, a grey-furred Beta named Thane, approaches you now in the sacred clearing.

"The moon has blessed you with transformation. But [i]how[/i] you shift—that is yours to discover. Some of us are wolf-only, running on four legs with the pack. Some are werewolves, walking upright as warriors. And a rare few..." He pauses. "...are Omega Variants. Dual-form. Both wolf and hybrid at will."

He studies you. "Tonight, in your trial, you will discover your true nature."

*choice
    #I feel the call of the four-legged hunt
        The wolf form calls to you—speed, agility, the pure joy of running with the pack.

        *set wolf_only true
        *set werewolf_only false
        *set omega_variant false

        "A wolf," Thane nods. "Swift. Cunning. The scouts and trackers of our kind."

        *set agility +20
        *set stealth +10
        *goto trial_begins

    #I feel power in the hybrid war-form
        The bipedal werewolf—massive, powerful, unstoppable. A living weapon.

        *set werewolf_only true
        *set wolf_only false
        *set omega_variant false

        "A werewolf," Thane says approvingly. "Our warriors. Our defenders."

        *set strength +20
        *set will +10
        *goto trial_begins

    #I feel both forms calling equally
        You sense it—the potential for [i]both[/i]. Wolf for speed, hybrid for strength. The rarest gift.

        *set omega_variant true
        *set wolf_only false
        *set werewolf_only false

        Thane's eyes widen. "Omega Variant. The moon has favored you greatly. With great power comes great trials."

        *set strength +10
        *set agility +10
        *set will +10
        *goto trial_begins

*label trial_begins

The full moon blazes overhead. The Whispering Pines Pack encircles you—dozens of wolves and werewolves, their eyes reflecting lunar fire.

Alpha Rurik, in his massive black wolf form, steps forward. His mental voice resonates in your skull:

[i]"The trial is simple: prove yourself. Hunt. Fight. Survive. Those who succeed join the pack. Those who fail... do not leave this forest."[/i]

He gestures with his muzzle toward the deep woods.

[i]"Your prey awaits. A corrupted beast—neither natural wolf nor lupine, but something twisted by dark magic. Kill it. Bring proof. Return before dawn."[/i]

*set lupine_relation +10

You shift—muscles rippling, bones crackling—into your lupine form. The world explodes into scent and sound.

*choice
    #Track the corrupted beast by scent
        Your lupine nose catches it immediately—wrongness on the wind. Decay and dark magic.

        *set cunning +10
        *goto hunt_sequence

    #Howl for guidance from the moon
        You tilt your head back and howl. The moon seems to answer—knowledge flooding your mind. The prey is northeast.

        *set will +10
        *goto hunt_sequence

    #Rush headlong into the hunt
        No time for subtlety. You charge into the darkness, trusting instinct.

        *set strength +10
        *set rage +5
        *goto hunt_sequence

*label hunt_sequence

*comment Condensed hunt for space - player tracks and finds the corrupted beast

You find it in a clearing—a massive, twisted thing. Once a wolf, now corrupted by Varkyr blood magic. An abomination.

It turns, snarling. The fight begins.

*choice
    *if (wolf_only)
        #Use wolf speed and agility

The corrupted beast lies dead. You take its head as proof.

As you stand over the corpse, bloodied and victorious, you realize: this is your first kill as a lupine.

Was it guilty? Corrupted, yes—but was it responsible for its corruption? Or was it a victim?
