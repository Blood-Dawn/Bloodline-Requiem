*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
*comment LUPINE ABILITIES - Lunar Power Selection System
*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
*comment Lupines choose abilities based on TYPE (Wolf/Werewolf/Omega)
*comment and RANK. All Lupines get common abilities automatically.
*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

*label lupine_ability_select
*comment Called when Lupine reaches a new rank

The moon calls to you with renewed strength. Your ascension to [b]${lupine_rank}[/b] deepens your connection to the primal hunt. Choose [b]two abilities[/b] befitting your nature.

*comment Track selections
*temp abilities_selected 0
*temp ability_choice_1 ""
*temp ability_choice_2 ""

*comment Determine lupine type from game state
*temp lupine_type ""
*if (omega_variant = true)
    *set lupine_type "Omega"
*elseif (werewolf_only = true)
    *set lupine_type "Werewolf"
*else
    *set lupine_type "Wolf"

*comment Route to appropriate rank tree
*if (lupine_rank = "Turned Omega") or (lupine_rank = "Omega")
    *goto omega_base_abilities
*elseif (lupine_rank = "Zeta Scout")
    *goto zeta_abilities
*elseif (lupine_rank = "Epsilon Forager") or (lupine_rank = "Epsilon Artisan")
    *goto epsilon_abilities
*elseif (lupine_rank = "Gamma Healer") or (lupine_rank = "Gamma Warder")
    *goto gamma_abilities
*elseif (lupine_rank = "Delta Emissary") or (lupine_rank = "Delta Sentinel")
    *goto delta_abilities
*elseif (lupine_rank = "Beta Guardian") or (lupine_rank = "Beta Councilor")
    *goto beta_abilities
*elseif (lupine_rank = "Alpha Commandant")
    *goto alpha_abilities
*elseif (lupine_rank = "Alpha Prime")
    *goto alpha_prime_abilities
*elseif (lupine_rank = "Alpha Sovereign")
    *goto alpha_sovereign_abilities
*elseif (lupine_rank = "Lunar Progenitor")
    *goto lunar_progenitor_abilities
*else
    *bug Unrecognized Lupine rank: ${lupine_rank}

*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
*comment SUBROUTINES
*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

*label record_lupine_selection
*params ability_name

*if (abilities_selected = 0)
    *set ability_choice_1 ability_name
    *set abilities_selected +1
*else
    *set ability_choice_2 ability_name
    *set abilities_selected +1
*return

*label apply_lupine_abilities
*line_break

*if (lupine_rank = "Lunar Progenitor")
    The transformation is absolute. You are the [b]Origin Wolf Reborn[/b]—legend made real.
*else
    You have mastered [b]${ability_choice_1}[/b] and [b]${ability_choice_2}[/b].

    The moon witnesses your growth. These powers are yours under her gaze.

*comment Update unlocked_abilities tracking string
*if (lupine_rank != "Lunar Progenitor")
    *set unlocked_abilities &(" " & ability_choice_1 & ", " & ability_choice_2 & ";")
*else
    *set unlocked_abilities &" ALL LUNAR PROGENITOR ABILITIES;"

*return
