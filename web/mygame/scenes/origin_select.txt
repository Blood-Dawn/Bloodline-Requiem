*label origin_select

The shadows of Veilfall stretch long across a world balanced on the edge of a knife. Varkyrs, Lupines, Forsaken, and Humans—all vying for power, survival, and the fragments of an ancient artifact that could reshape reality itself.

But every legend begins with a choice. Every bloodline has an origin. Every shadow was once cast by light.

[b]Who are you?[/b]

*line_break

*goto choose_gender

*label choose_gender

*if (gender != "unspecified")
    *goto origin_menu

Before the shadows claim your path, choose how you identify. This will also set whether titles use masculine or feminine forms when they apply.

*choice
    #Male
        *set gender "male"
        *set gender_title_style "masculine"
        *goto origin_menu
    #Female
        *set gender "female"
        *set gender_title_style "feminine"
        *goto origin_menu
    #Other (enter your own)
        *goto gender_other_input

*label gender_other_input

State your gender in your own words.
*input_text gender_custom
*set gender gender_custom

Choose which set of titles you prefer when gendered ranks appear.
*choice
    #Use masculine titles (Baron, Duke)
        *set gender_title_style "masculine"
        *goto origin_menu
    #Use feminine titles (Baroness, Duchess)
        *set gender_title_style "feminine"
        *goto origin_menu
