[b]Achievements Overview[/b]

- forsaken_ngplus: Unlock by completing the campaign (set in COMPLETION_BANNER).
- assassin_initiate: Call *achieve when the player completes their first Guild contract.
- crimson_initiate: Award after the Crimson Spire initiation finishes.
- moon_trial: Award when the Moon-Born Trial succeeds.
- veil_apprentice: Award on successful response to the observatory alarm.
