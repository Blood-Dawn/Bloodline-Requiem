The vaulted hall of the Silver Legion buzzes with disciplined chatter as recruits file in. Sunlight streams through high windows, glinting off polished armor and banners emblazoned with the imperial crest. You stand among dozens of new inductees—humans who have chosen to fight the supernatural threat.

At the front, Inquisitor Vale commands the room with his mere presence. Scarred, gray-haired, eyes like chips of ice. He has hunted vampires and werewolves for thirty years. He has [i]survived[/i] for thirty years—a miracle in itself.

"You are here," he begins without preamble, "because the monsters are winning."

Shocked murmurs ripple through the crowd.

"The Veil Protocol keeps most humans ignorant of the supernatural world. But we—the Silver Legion—know the truth. Vampires feed in our streets. Werewolves prowl our forests. And now, hybrids—Forsaken—multiply in the shadows."
