The catacombs beneath Veilfall swallow all sound, their narrow passages thick with the scent of damp stone and lingering death. This is 123 Street Underground—one of the Forsaken's primary gathering places, hidden beneath the poorest quarter of the city.

You descend stone stairs worn smooth by centuries of footfalls. Each step takes you deeper into the realm of outcasts and hybrids—beings rejected by both Varkyr and Lupine alike.

*label the_underground

The underground chamber opens before you—a cathedral carved from living rock. Hundreds of Forsaken fill the space, their features a disturbing blend of vampire and wolf. Some have fangs and fur. Others possess blood-red eyes and lupine claws. Each is unique. Each is an abomination in the eyes of the pure bloodlines.

At the center, atop a raised platform of ancient stone, stands Caius the Unbound.

He is the first. The original Forsaken—created during the Millennium War when a Varkyr and Lupine bloodline were forcibly merged through dark ritual. Where others show unstable features, Caius is perfect: vampire grace and lupine power seamlessly combined.

His voice carries without shouting. "Brothers. Sisters. Children of two bloodlines and heirs to neither."

The crowd falls silent.

"We gather tonight because the time of hiding is ending. The Lupines and Varkyrs war over scraps of territory while treating us as vermin. But we are not vermin. We are the [i]future[/i]."

*label caius_speech

Caius continues, his words building in intensity.

"The Crimson Spire views us as failed experiments. The Whispering Pines see us as diseased mongrels. Even humans hunt us with more fervor than they hunt pure-bloods, because our very existence proves that the boundaries between species are not as fixed as they wish to believe."

Murmurs of agreement ripple through the crowd.

"But I have discovered something. An artifact lost during the Millennium War—the Ecliptic Amulet. Legend claims it can manipulate the Veil itself, the barrier that separates supernatural beings from mortal awareness."

Your attention sharpens. The same amulet the Varkyrs and Lupines seek.

"But what the pure-bloods do not know," Caius says, his smile turning predatory, "is that the amulet has a [i]third[/i] power. It can stabilize hybrid forms. With the Ecliptic Amulet, we Forsaken would no longer suffer the instability that plagues us. No more pain. No more madness. No more slow death as our bloodlines war within our flesh."

*set ecliptic_amulet_known true

The crowd erupts in excited whispers.

"I have found the first clue to its location," Caius announces. "Ancient texts recovered from Millennium War ruins. They speak of a [i]test[/i]—a trial designed to identify those worthy of wielding the amulet's power."

He pauses, letting anticipation build.

"I need agents to undergo this trial. Those brave enough to risk everything for our people's salvation."

"The trial," Caius explains, "is three-fold. Physical prowess. Mental fortitude. And the ability to master your dual nature without succumbing to instability."

He produces a map marked with three locations in Veilfall.

"Each location holds a fragment of the ancient text that reveals the amulet's hiding place. But each is guarded—by traps, by enemies, or by your own inner demons."

He combines the fragments with those retrieved by the other survivors. Ancient text becomes legible.

"The Sunken Cathedral," Caius reads. "Buried beneath Veilfall during the Millennium War. The amulet waits there, protected by the final trial."
