The Millennium War reshaped Veilfall as factions clashed for dominance and survival. The Lupines fought to keep balance with nature, the Varkyrs schemed from the shadows, and the Forsaken sought forbidden power.

Key figures emerged from each faction, such as Aeliana who united the packs and Valerius who founded the Night Council. Their legacies still influence the uneasy peace today.
