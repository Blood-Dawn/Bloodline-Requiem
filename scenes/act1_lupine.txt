Legends tell that only those who confront their inner beasts emerge stronger, earning the right to stand among the pack as equals.

Tonight the air is thick with the scent of pine and anticipation. The rustle of leaves is punctuated by distant howls, guiding you deeper into the forest. Each step is a test of resolve; each shadow seems to pulse with the energy of ages-old rites. Before long, the path opens into a clearing where a great bonfire roars, its flames licking toward the heavens. The pack gathers there, silent silhouettes under the moonlight. It is here that you must prove yourself, for success means acceptance—and failure, exile or worse.
