*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
*comment MAGE ABILITIES - Spell Selection System
*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
*comment Mages pick two new spells each time they rank up.

*label mage_ability_select
*temp abilities_selected 0
*temp ability_choice_1 ""
*temp ability_choice_2 ""

Your rank as [b]${mage_rank}[/b] unlocks deeper arcane patterns. Choose [b]two spells[/b] to inscribe into your grimoire.

*if (mage_rank = "Apprentice")
    *goto apprentice_spells
*elseif (mage_rank = "Journeymage")
    *goto journeymage_spells
*elseif ((mage_rank = "Battlemage") or (mage_rank = "Spellwright"))
    *goto specialist_spells
*elseif (mage_rank = "Arcanist")
    *goto arcanist_spells
*elseif (mage_rank = "Archmage")
    *goto archmage_spells
*else
    *comment Fallback for unexpected rank
    *bug Unrecognized mage rank: ${mage_rank}

*comment Shared tracking helpers
*label record_selection
*params ability_name
*set abilities_selected +1
*if (abilities_selected = 1)
    *set ability_choice_1 ability_name
*elseif (abilities_selected = 2)
    *set ability_choice_2 ability_name

*if (abilities_selected >= 2)
    *goto ability_selection_complete
*return

*label apprentice_spells
[b]Apprentice Spells[/b] - Fundamentals every mage must master.

Choose two:

*choice
    *if (not arcane_bolt)
        #Arcane Bolt
            [i]Condense raw aether into a guided projectile.[/i]

            Reliable ranged attack; can disrupt lesser rituals.
            *set arcane_bolt true
            *gosub record_selection "Arcane Bolt"
            *goto check_apprentice_complete
    *if (not mage_sight)
        #Mage Sight
            [i]See the flows of the Veil and trace hidden sigils.[/i]

            Reveals magical traps, disguises, and resonance trails.
            *set mage_sight true
            *gosub record_selection "Mage Sight"
            *goto check_apprentice_complete
    *if (not wardweave)
        #Wardweave
            [i]Stitch a quick ward to blunt a single strike or spell.[/i]

            Grants brief damage reduction; stacks with other defenses.
            *set wardweave true
            *gosub record_selection "Wardweave"
            *goto check_apprentice_complete
    *if (not focus_mind)
        #Focus Mind
            [i]Breathe mana through your nerves, sharpening concentration.[/i]

            Reduces burnout, improves ritual control under stress.
            *set focus_mind true
            *gosub record_selection "Focus Mind"
            *goto check_apprentice_complete

*label check_apprentice_complete
*if (abilities_selected < 2)
    *goto apprentice_spells
*goto ability_selection_complete

*label journeymage_spells
[b]Journeymage Spells[/b] - Fieldwork essentials.

Choose two:

*choice
    *if (not blink_step)
        #Blink Step
            [i]Slip a few strides through folded space.[/i]

            Evade attacks, close gaps, or bypass barriers in a blink.
            *set blink_step true
            *gosub record_selection "Blink Step"
            *goto check_journeymage_complete
    *if (not aegis_sigil)
        #Aegis Sigil
            [i]Anchor a shield glyph that blooms into a dome on impact.[/i]

            Absorbs one heavy blow for you or an ally before fading.
            *set aegis_sigil true
            *gosub record_selection "Aegis Sigil"
            *goto check_journeymage_complete
    *if (not mana_flare)
        #Mana Flare
            [i]Overcharge your next spell with volatile aether.[/i]

            Boosts spell potency at the cost of temporary burnout.
            *set mana_flare true
            *gosub record_selection "Mana Flare"
            *goto check_journeymage_complete
    *if (not veil_pierce)
        #Veil Pierce
            [i]Cut through illusions and shadow veils.[/i]

            Nullifies basic glamour, reveals hidden doorways and portals.
            *set veil_pierce true
            *gosub record_selection "Veil Pierce"
            *goto check_journeymage_complete

*label check_journeymage_complete
*if (abilities_selected < 2)
    *goto journeymage_spells
*goto ability_selection_complete

*label specialist_spells
[b]Specialist Spells[/b] - Tools favored by Battlemages and Spellwrights.

Choose two:

*choice
    *if (not storm_nova)
        #Storm Nova
            [i]Release a ring of crackling force around you.[/i]

            Staggers foes and disrupts unstable rituals within range.
            *set storm_nova true
            *gosub record_selection "Storm Nova"
            *goto check_specialist_complete
    *if (not spellshield_dome)
        #Spellshield Dome
            [i]Erect a sustained barrier fueled by steady mana flow.[/i]

            Protects an area against projectiles and minor spells while channeled.
            *set spellshield_dome true
            *gosub record_selection "Spellshield Dome"
            *goto check_specialist_complete
    *if (not rune_trap)
        #Rune Trap
            [i]Lay a hidden sigil that detonates into binding chains.[/i]

            Excellent for ambushes or slowing fast targets.
            *set rune_trap true
            *gosub record_selection "Rune Trap"
            *goto check_specialist_complete
    *if (not etheric_chains)
        #Etheric Chains
            [i]Project bindings of condensed mana to immobilize a foe.[/i]

            Holds most targets briefly; stronger when cast from stealth.
            *set etheric_chains true
            *gosub record_selection "Etheric Chains"
            *goto check_specialist_complete

*label check_specialist_complete
*if (abilities_selected < 2)
    *goto specialist_spells
*goto ability_selection_complete

*label arcanist_spells
[b]Arcanist Spells[/b] - Mastery of space and time.

Choose two:

*choice
    *if (not temporal_anchor)
        #Temporal Anchor
            [i]Fix a moment in place; snap back to it when overwhelmed.[/i]

            Acts as a personal save-point during encounters.
            *set temporal_anchor true
            *gosub record_selection "Temporal Anchor"
            *goto check_arcanist_complete
    *if (not rift_step)
        #Rift Step
            [i]Tear a short-lived rift to bypass walls or appear behind foes.[/i]

            Risky but invaluable for infiltration.
            *set rift_step true
            *gosub record_selection "Rift Step"
            *goto check_arcanist_complete
    *if (not soul_ward)
        #Soul Ward
            [i]Shield the mind and spirit from domination or terror magic.[/i]

            Grants resistance to mental influence and fear effects.
            *set soul_ward true
            *gosub record_selection "Soul Ward"
            *goto check_arcanist_complete
    *if (not cataclysmic_arc)
        #Cataclysmic Arc
            [i]Channel a devastating beam that drains your mana reserve.[/i]

            Ends most fights—but risks total burnout if overused.
            *set cataclysmic_arc true
            *gosub record_selection "Cataclysmic Arc"
            *goto check_arcanist_complete

*label check_arcanist_complete
*if (abilities_selected < 2)
    *goto arcanist_spells
*goto ability_selection_complete

*label archmage_spells
[b]Archmage Invocations[/b] - Apex spells few dare to touch.

Choose two:

*choice
    *if (not temporal_anchor)
        #Temporal Anchor
            *goto select_archmage_anchor
    *if (not rift_step)
        #Rift Step
            *goto select_archmage_rift
    *if (not soul_ward)
        #Soul Ward
            *goto select_archmage_soul
    *if (not cataclysmic_arc)
        #Cataclysmic Arc
            *goto select_archmage_arc
    #Focus on refining existing spells instead
        [i]Deepen your understanding rather than add new tools.[/i]

        Increase mana efficiency and ward stability slightly.
        *set mana +5
        *set ward_stability +5
        *gosub record_selection "Refined repertoire"
        *goto check_archmage_complete

*label select_archmage_anchor
Temporal Anchor becomes near-instant—no chant required.
*set temporal_anchor true
*gosub record_selection "Temporal Anchor (Perfected)"
*goto check_archmage_complete

*label select_archmage_rift
Rift Step extends farther and can pull one ally with you.
*set rift_step true
*gosub record_selection "Rift Step (Extended)"
*goto check_archmage_complete

*label select_archmage_soul
Soul Ward can now shelter nearby allies when cast.
*set soul_ward true
*gosub record_selection "Soul Ward (Shared)"
*goto check_archmage_complete

*label select_archmage_arc
Cataclysmic Arc costs slightly less mana and leaves a lingering burn.
*set cataclysmic_arc true
*gosub record_selection "Cataclysmic Arc (Refined)"
*goto check_archmage_complete

*label check_archmage_complete
*if (abilities_selected < 2)
    *goto archmage_spells

*label ability_selection_complete

You inscribe ${ability_choice_1} and ${ability_choice_2} into your spellbook. Power hums, eager to be tested.

*return
