*label caius_entry
[b]CAIUS THE UNBOUND[/b]  
Role: Founder of the Forsaken Enclave  
Age: Unknown (over a millennium)  

- First successful hybrid, created when Varkyr and Lupine essences were forcibly merged during the Millennium War.  
- Seeks to stabilize hybrid transformations and prove the Forsaken deserve a place in the world.  
- Pragmatic and patient; values those who choose their path rather than stumble into it.  
- Covets the Forsaken shard of the Ecliptic Amulet to end the dependence on either parent bloodline.  
- Considers the player a potential heir-or rival-if they embrace hybridity.

*label rurik_entry
[b]RURIK BLACKFANG[/b]  
Role: Alpha of the Whispering Pines Pack  
Age: 38 (turned at 25)  

- Leads with a blend of ferocity and honor; despises senseless cruelty.  
- Believes controlling the Amulet's lunar aspect will free his pack from forced transformations.  
- Respects strength and candor; distrusts those who hide behind titles.  
- Open to alliances with humans who prove courage-or with hybrids who respect pack law.  
- His bite can grant Lupine status; his trust is harder to earn.

*label isabella_entry
[b]VISCOUNTESS ISABELLA CRIMSON[/b]  
Role: Varkyr diplomat and Matriarch's envoy  
Age: 247  

- Master negotiator who prefers bargains to open war, yet has ordered countless discreet assassinations.  
- Uses charm and menace interchangeably; maintains the Spire's ties to wealthy humans like Adrian Blackwell.  
- Views the player as a potential asset to the Crimson Spire, especially if they demonstrate political acumen.  
- Interested in using the Amulet to solidify Varkyr dominance while preventing a second Millennium War.

*label vale_entry
[b]INQUISITOR VALE[/b]  
Role: Commander within the Silver Legion  
Age: 52  

- Veteran monster hunter; carries scars from battles with Varkyrs, Lupines, and Forsaken.  
- Ruthless pragmatist who believes secrecy saves more lives than open war.  
- Formed a special task force to chase rumors of the Ecliptic Amulet and rogue elements like Captain Thorne.  
- Sees noble-born recruits as leverage in court, and commoners as proof that anyone can fight back.

[b]ELENA CROSS[/b]  
Role: Veteran Sentinel of the Silver Legion  
Age: 31  

- Partnered with the player in the Human Act 1 route; tactical, dry-humored, dependable under fire.  
- Survived an ambush by rogue Varkyrs, making her wary of diplomacy without leverage.  
- Advocates for using the Amulet defensively rather than destroying it outright.  
- Values loyalty and preparation; distrusts reckless heroics.

[b]CAPTAIN THORNE[/b]  
Role: Former Silver Legion officer, now rogue zealot  
Age: 45  

- Believes the Amulet should be used to annihilate all supernaturals-even if it risks collateral damage.  
- Commands fanatics willing to stage attacks to provoke war between factions.  
- Acts as primary antagonist in the Human Act 1 route; tests whether the player chooses duty, mercy, or independence.  
- Obsessed with erasing his past failures through extreme action.

*label adrian_entry
[b]ADRIAN BLACKWELL[/b]  
Role: Merchant lord and human liaison to the supernatural  
Age: 36  

- Facilitates discreet blood trade with the Spire; funds Legion expeditions; profits from both sides.  
- Values deals over dogma; will back whoever secures his family's safety and fortune.  
- Holds ledgers with evidence of Night Council manipulation and Amulet shard movements.  
- Potential ally or threat depending on how the player handles noble politics.
