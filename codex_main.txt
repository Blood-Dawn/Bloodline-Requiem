[b]The Ecliptic Amulet[/b]

Born from the catastrophic overload of the ancient Veilstone during the Millennium War, the Ecliptic Amulet became a vessel of unimaginable power—and unimaginable instability.

When the Progenitor and the Origin Wolf fell in their final duel, the backlash shattered the Veilstone into four fragments, each attuned to one of the major powers:

• [b]Human Shard[/b] – Held by the Crown of Veilfall. Enhances mortal resilience and nullifies certain supernatural influence.

• [b]Varkyr Shard[/b] – Kept within the Crimson Spire. Amplifies blood magic and extends dominion over shadows.

• [b]Lupine Shard[/b] – Guarded by the Whispering Pines Pack. Strengthens lunar connection and pack unity.

• [b]Forsaken Shard[/b] – Claimed by Caius the Unbound. Grants hybrid transformation stability and the ability to turn others into Forsaken.

Legends say that reuniting all four shards could either restore the ancient balance... or unleash a second cataclysm that would make the Millennium War seem a mere prelude.
