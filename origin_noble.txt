*label study_discovery

The study door creaks. Your father's body lies slumped over his desk, skin pale, twin punctures at his throat. Papers are scattered around a shattered decanter-the scent of spiced blood heavy in the air.

A single black rose rests atop the ledger. Beside it, a signet ring engraved with a stylized spire: the mark of the Crimson Spire's Viscountess Isabella Crimson. A calculated message.

Footsteps whisper behind you. Isabella herself steps from the shadows, crimson eyes gleaming. "My condolences, ${rank}. Your father's debts have... matured."

*set met_Isabella true
*set Isabella_relation +10

*choice
    #"You murdered him. Why?"
        "He traded secrets to the Lupines and the Church," Isabella says. "Breaking the Veil. I removed a liability-and offer you protection in exchange."

        *set will +5
        *goto spire_offer

    #"House Ravencrest bends knee to no vampire."
        Isabella smiles, sharp. "Then bend to extinction? The Night Council already circles. Choose quickly."

        *set will +10
        *goto spire_offer

    #"We negotiate. House Ravencrest still has value."
        "Clever," she purrs. "Perhaps you'll survive longer than your sire."

        *set cunning +5
        *goto spire_offer

*label spire_offer

Isabella's proposal is simple: accept the Embrace and secure your house under Spire protection-or refuse and risk annihilation from enemies you cannot see. The Ecliptic Amulet's human shard is in play; nobles without supernatural backing will be pawns at best.

*choice
    #Accept the Embrace to claim power
        Cold fangs pierce your wrist. Fire floods your veins, burning away mortality.

        *set alignment "varkyrs"
        *set rank "Commoner"
        *set Turned true

        Isabella wipes your lips. "Rise, ${rank}. You belong to the night now. Serve the Spire, and your House will flourish."
        *goto noble_departure_varkyr

    #Refuse and gather human allies instead

        *set alignment "human"
