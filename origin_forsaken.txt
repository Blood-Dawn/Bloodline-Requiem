Seven days ago, you were dying.

A Varkyr attacked you in Veilfall's alleys—drained you nearly dry. As you lay broken, a Lupine pack passed through, and one—young, reckless—bit you, thinking to turn you.

The two venoms should have killed each other. Should have killed [i]you[/i].

Instead, they [i]merged[/i].

*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
*comment CAIUS THE UNBOUND - First Meeting
*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

*label caius_appears

You wake in darkness. Not death—though you came close. The conflicting magics in your blood war for dominance, ripping you apart from the inside.

Then—a voice. Deep, measured, carrying authority that makes even your warring essences pause:

"Fascinating. You should be dead. Vampiric venom and lupine infection destroy each other on contact. Yet here you are. [i]Changing[/i]."

A figure steps from shadow. Tall—6'7" easily. Silver hair despite an ageless face. And eyes... gods, those eyes. One crimson like a Varkyr's. One amber like a Lupine's.

"I am Caius," he says. "Caius the Unbound. And you, dying hybrid, have a choice."

*set met_Caius true

He kneels beside you. "I can stabilize the transformation. Make you Forsaken—neither Varkyr nor Lupine, but [i]both[/i]. You will have powers neither bloodline alone can claim. You will also be hunted by both. Hated. Feared."

He produces a vial of shimmering liquid—blood and moonlight somehow mixed.

"Or I can let you die here. Painlessly. Your choice."

*choice
    #"Save me. I'll become Forsaken."
        "Good," Caius says. "Survival is the first wisdom."

        *set will +15
        *set forsaken_relation +20
        *goto ritual_begins

    #"What's the cost? Nothing is free."
        Caius smiles—a predator's expression. "Smart. The cost is loyalty. You will join my enclave. Serve until you prove yourself worthy of independence. Years, perhaps. But you'll be [i]alive[/i]."

        *set cunning +15
        *set forsaken_relation +10
        *goto ritual_begins

    #"I... I don't want to be a monster."
        "Too late," Caius says, not unkindly. "You're already transforming. The only question is whether you do so with guidance... or as a mindless abomination I'll have to put down."

        His heterochromatic gaze holds yours. "Choose quickly. You have minutes."

        *set control +10
        *goto forced_choice

*label forced_choice
Survival wins. "Do it," you gasp.

*goto ritual_begins

*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
*comment THE RITE OF CONVERGENCE
*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

*label ritual_begins

Caius lifts you effortlessly, carrying you deeper into what you now recognize as the Forsaken Underground—the tunnel network beneath Veilfall.

A chamber opens before you, carved with symbols that hurt to look at. Other Forsaken watch from the shadows—dozens of them, all bearing the telltale signs of hybrid nature.

Caius lays you on an altar beneath an opening to the sky. The full moon blazes overhead.

"The Rite of Convergence," he announces, "will force blood and moon to [i]cooperate[/i] rather than war. It will hurt. Scream if you must. But if you surrender to the pain, you die."

He pours the vial's contents into your mouth.

Reality [i]explodes[/i].

*page_break

*comment Condensed ritual sequence for space

The agony is beyond description. Your bones break and reform. Your blood boils and freezes simultaneously. You feel fangs extend and retract. Claws emerge from your fingertips.

And through it all, Caius's voice, anchoring you: "You are neither. You are [i]both[/i]. Accept it. Master it."

Seven hours later, as dawn approaches, the pain recedes.

You stand—transformed. Hybrid. Forsaken.

*set hybrid_stability +30
*gosub_scene rank_progression gain_xp 75 "Survived the Rite of Convergence"

Caius studies you. "The transformation stabilized. Now we determine your orientation. Are you Vamp-Wolf—vampiric core with lupine secondary? Or Wolf-Vamp—lupine core with vampiric secondary?"

He gestures for you to focus inward.

    #I feel the wolf's primal fury stronger
        The moon's call drowns out the blood-thirst. You are beast first, predator second.

        *set forsaken_type "Wolf-Vamp"
        *set rage +20
        *set Bloodlust -10
        *set strength +15

        "Wolf-Vamp," Caius says. "Physical prowess will be your strength. Silver will hurt more. Sunlight less."

        *goto abilities_unlock

        "Balanced," Caius's eyes gleam. "Rare. Very rare. You may unlock War Form sooner than most."

        *goto abilities_unlock

Power settles into you. The abilities are yours.

Caius extends his hand. "Welcome to the Forsaken. You are hated by the world above. Down here, you are [i]kin[/i]."

Other Forsaken approach—some offering nods, others wary but curious. You are one of them now.

*label first_lesson

Over the next nights, Caius teaches you the basics:

"Varkyrs will see you as corrupted. Lupines will see you as tainted. Humans will see you as double-threat. All will try to kill you."

He shows you how to shift—not just between human and hybrid form, but how to suppress your Varkyr or Lupine traits to pass among each faction.

"Subterfuge is survival. But never forget: you are not lesser than them. You are [i]more[/i]."

He gestures to the tunnels. "The Forsaken Enclave is your sanctuary. But you cannot hide forever. Eventually, you must walk the surface. Face the factions. Prove we deserve to exist."
