*comment VARKYR ABILITIES - Blood Magic Selection System
*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

*label varkyr_ability_select
*comment Called when Varkyr reaches a new rank

The blood sings with newfound power. Your ascension to [b]${rank}[/b] unlocks deeper mysteries of the sanguine arts. Choose [b]two abilities[/b] to master from those befitting your station.

*comment Track selections
*temp abilities_selected 0
*temp ability_choice_1 ""
*temp ability_choice_2 ""
*temp newly_unlocked ""

*comment Align gendered ranks with player title preference so pronouns stay consistent
*if ((gender_title_style = "feminine") or ((gender_title_style = "unspecified") and (gender = "female")))
    *if (rank = "Baron")
        *set rank "Baroness"
    *if (rank = "Viscount")
        *set rank "Viscountess"
    *if (rank = "Count")
        *set rank "Countess"
    *if (rank = "Marquis")
        *set rank "Marchioness"
    *if (rank = "Duke")
        *set rank "Duchess"
    *if (rank = "Archduke")
        *set rank "Archduchess"
*elseif ((gender_title_style = "masculine") or ((gender_title_style = "unspecified") and (gender = "male")))
    *if (rank = "Baroness")
        *set rank "Baron"
    *if (rank = "Viscountess")
        *set rank "Viscount"
    *if (rank = "Countess")
        *set rank "Count"
    *if (rank = "Marchioness")
        *set rank "Marquis"
    *if (rank = "Duchess")
        *set rank "Duke"
    *if (rank = "Archduchess")
        *set rank "Archduke"

*comment Route to appropriate rank tree
*if (rank = "Commoner")
    *goto commoner_abilities
*elseif (rank = "Knight")
    *goto knight_abilities
*elseif (rank = "Baron") or (rank = "Baroness")
    *goto baron_abilities
*elseif (rank = "Viscount") or (rank = "Viscountess")
    *goto viscount_abilities
*elseif (rank = "Count") or (rank = "Countess")
    *goto count_abilities
*elseif (rank = "Marquis") or (rank = "Marchioness")
    *goto marquis_abilities
*elseif (rank = "Duke") or (rank = "Duchess")
    *goto duke_abilities
*elseif (rank = "Archduke") or (rank = "Archduchess")
    *goto archduke_abilities
*elseif (rank = "Imperial")
    *goto imperial_abilities
*elseif (rank = "Progenitor")
    *goto progenitor_abilities
*else
    *comment Fallback for unrecognized rank
    *bug Unrecognized Varkyr rank: ${rank}

*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
*comment SUBROUTINES
*comment ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

*label record_selection
*params ability_name

*if (abilities_selected = 0)
    *set ability_choice_1 ability_name
    *set abilities_selected +1
*else
    *set ability_choice_2 ability_name
    *set abilities_selected +1
*return

*label apply_varkyr_abilities
*line_break

*if (rank = "Progenitor")
    The transformation is complete. You stand as the pinnacle of vampiric evolution—the [b]Progenitor Reborn[/b].
*else
    You have mastered [b]${ability_choice_1}[/b] and [b]${ability_choice_2}[/b].

    The blood remembers. These powers are yours eternally.

*comment Update unlocked_abilities tracking string
*if (rank != "Progenitor")
    *set newly_unlocked " "
    *set newly_unlocked & ability_choice_1
    *set newly_unlocked & ", "
    *set newly_unlocked & ability_choice_2
    *set newly_unlocked & ";"
    *set unlocked_abilities & newly_unlocked
*else
    *set unlocked_abilities & " ALL PROGENITOR ABILITIES;"

*return
